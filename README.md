# Tars SDR assignment — US Senior Living

## Territory

- **Vertical:** Healthcare → Senior Living
- **Geography:** United States
- **Primary use case:** Customer Acquisition
- **Primary buyers:** CMO, Chief Marketing Officer, SVP/VP Marketing, Head of Marketing
- **Secondary buyers:** senior sales, digital marketing, growth and customer-experience leaders where their remit is materially connected to lead generation, qualification, conversion, tours or occupancy.

## Completion status

**Research complete: 20 accounts / 40 contacts.** Outreach sequences are intentionally not included in this stage.

## Research standard

- Facts are cited to public primary sources whenever available.
- Sales hypotheses are labeled as hypotheses; they are not claims about the account.
- Titles and current employment are verified on current official company leadership pages or contemporary official announcements.
- No private contact details are guessed or included.

## Account-selection criteria

Accounts were selected for a combination of multi-community scale, a US consumer-facing site, an observable inquiry/location/tour journey, relevant senior marketing/sales leadership, and evidence that occupancy, tours, lead generation, sales conversion or scalable growth is commercially important.

## Contact-selection criteria

Each account contains exactly two distinct, publicly verified current leaders. Priority was given to senior marketing and sales owners. Where only one clear marketing leader was publicly identifiable, the second contact is a clearly relevant executive accountable for customer experience, operations, growth or community conversion.

## Methodology

1. Start with official company leadership pages, current role announcements, annual reports, job descriptions and consumer sites.
2. Record only facts directly supported by linked sources; distinguish historical scale figures from current figures.
3. Mark public LinkedIn URLs as **Not publicly verified** rather than guessing a profile URL.
4. State a narrowly testable **Sales Hypothesis** for each account. Hypotheses are not claims about the company.
5. Use Tars' published customer-acquisition capabilities only to connect a verified acquisition journey to a proposed test.

## Limitations

- Some private operators do not publish current unit, community or occupancy counts. Those values are not inferred.
- Where a company only exposes a single marketing executive, a secondary commercial/operations/customer-experience leader is used and their relevance is stated.
- No email addresses or phone numbers are included or inferred.

## Contents

- `accounts/`: account research and sources.
- `email-sequences/`, `linkedin-sequences/`, and `call-scripts/`: reserved for the next stage; deliberately empty.

Tars product capabilities are cited to [Tars' customer-acquisition page](https://hellotars.com/customer-acquisition-ai). Any performance claim in outreach must be framed as a pilot hypothesis and measured against the account's present funnel; it is not represented as a guaranteed outcome.
