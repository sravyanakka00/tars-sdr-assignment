# Brookdale Senior Living

## Facts

- Brookdale operates 500+ retirement communities serving approximately 46,000 residents across 41 states (company-reported as of May 31, 2026). [Source](https://www.brookdale.com/en/our-company.html)
- It provides independent living, assisted living, memory care, and CCRCs. [Source](https://s26.q4cdn.com/858530099/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/Brookdale-2025-Annual-Report.pdf)
- Brookdale's 2025 annual report says it uses local and national marketing, digital advertising and a Connection Center; online forms and many calls are handled by trained senior-living advisors who schedule community visits. [Source](https://s26.q4cdn.com/858530099/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/Brookdale-2025-Annual-Report.pdf)
- Its 2026 investor presentation identifies occupancy growth at owned communities below 80% as a significant operating-income opportunity. [Source](https://s26.q4cdn.com/858530099/files/doc_events/2026/May/07/1Q2026InvestorPresentation.pdf)

## Website/customer-acquisition journey

Visitors can explore location and care options, call, submit an online inquiry, and schedule a community visit. The Connection Center is the stated handoff point for online forms and many calls. [Source](https://s26.q4cdn.com/858530099/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/Brookdale-2025-Annual-Report.pdf)

## Why customer acquisition matters

Brookdale publicly links targeted sales and marketing to increasing move-ins and occupancy, and centralizes a material share of its inbound response through the Connection Center. [Source](https://s26.q4cdn.com/858530099/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/Brookdale-2025-Annual-Report.pdf)

## Tars relevance
