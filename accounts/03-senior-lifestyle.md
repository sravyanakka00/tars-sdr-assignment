## Why customer acquisition matters

The company has a national brand and a structured, multi-field inquiry flow. Helping families identify the correct care setting before community routing could make that first conversion more useful.

## Tars relevance

Tars can guide a visitor through qualification and route context to sales rather than collecting only a form submission. [Source](https://hellotars.com/customer-acquisition-ai)
